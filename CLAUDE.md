# CLAUDE.md — vr-racing

Personal static site (not MEcommerce). One file, `index.html`, plus `img/`, `og.png` and `tools/`; custom domain `vrracing.commercedwar.com`.

Rules: keep it a single dependency-free page (fonts from Google Fonts only, loaded non-blocking; imagery is real photography and official game/product frames — never drawn or stock art); **no analytics and no forms that store data** (analytics declined 9 Sept 2026, to be revisited on evidence of no bookings — the plan for it is in `README.md` "Analytics") — booking goes through the WhatsApp deep link; every price, rule or offer change is the owner's call and is reflected in `README.md`. **The launch offer's state lives only in the three `data-offer*` attributes on `<body>`** (README "The launch offer"); never invent a count, and never show a number the owner has not set. **Privacy is a hard rule:** no personal name, personal phone number or street address anywhere in the repo (Sector 38 only; the WhatsApp number is the business one, in `<body data-wa>`; no map pin); images go through `tools/optimise_images.py`, which strips metadata, and nothing in a room photograph may identify the building. No emoji on the page (the site's own marks do that job). Run `tools/verify.cjs` before pushing (README "Checks"). Commit prefixes as in the workspace (`feat:`, `fix:`, `docs:`, `chore:`).

Design brief and decisions: `Claude generated files\three-tasks-2026-09-03\ANSWERS-2026-09-04.md`, `Claude generated files\vr-racing-round-2\`, and — for the conversion rework of 9 Sept 2026 — `Claude generated files\vr-racing-brief-2026-09-09\` (the brief, the plan, four Jason reviews, the baseline, the report).
